**Tips for choosing**:
- Quantum talent is scarce and competitive — expect focus on PhDs, post-docs, and rare hardware/software specialists. Specialized firms offer better networks and understanding of the technical nuances (e.g., different qubit modalities).
- Many agencies have strong UK/Europe presence due to major quantum hubs (e.g., UK National Quantum Technologies Programme), but several serve the US market (California, Boston, New York, etc.).
- Check for current openings on their sites, and verify track record via testimonials or community feedback (e.g., Reddit’s r/QuantumComputing).
- For executive/leadership or high-volume needs, combine with broader executive search firms.
